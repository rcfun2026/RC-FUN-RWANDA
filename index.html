About RC Fun Rwanda

RC Fun Rwanda is a leading RC hobby and entertainment company based in Kigali. We offer exciting remote-control cars, trucks, accessories, competitions, training sessions and family events.

What is RC?

RC stands for Remote Control. RC vehicles are controlled wirelessly using a transmitter. They combine technology, engineering and fun.


Benefits of RC Hobbies:

✔ Improves hand-eye coordination
✔ Builds technology skills
✔ Encourages creativity
✔ Develops problem-solving abilities
✔ Provides exciting entertainment
